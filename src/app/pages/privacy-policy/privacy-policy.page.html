Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

This application is designed to provide a simple and enjoyable puzzle experience. Your privacy is important, and the game is built to minimize data collection.

The game does not require registration and does not collect personal identity information such as name, email address, or contact details.

Information Stored Locally

The application uses local storage on your device to save game-related data. This allows your progress and preferences to remain available between sessions.

  • Best score achieved
  • Current game score
  • Total tile merges
  • Selected grid size
  • Sound preference

All stored information remains on your device and is not transmitted externally.

No Account Required

This application does not require account creation or login. You can play the game immediately without providing any personal data.

No Tracking Technologies

The game does not use cookies for tracking purposes and does not integrate analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or behavioral tracking systems.

No usage analytics or personal behavior data is collected.

Data Security

All data is stored locally in your browser or device storage. The developer does not have access to your game data.

Clearing browser storage, clearing app data, or uninstalling the application may permanently remove saved progress.

Children's Privacy

This game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or minors. Since no personal data is collected, the application is safe for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

The privacy policy may be updated to reflect improvements or changes in the application. Updates will be reflected within the app.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or the application, please contact the developer through the platform where the application is distributed.
